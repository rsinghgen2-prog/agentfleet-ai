# AgentFleet AI - Project Overview

## Business Concept

**AgentFleet AI** is a comprehensive AI automation platform designed for small and medium businesses. Instead of hiring multiple employees, businesses can deploy an AI Fleet that handles:

- Customer support (24/7)
- Appointment scheduling
- Lead qualification
- Sales follow-up
- CRM automation
- Email management
- Analytics and reporting
- Operational workflows

**Tagline:** "Your AI Workforce, Ready on Day One."

- Small to medium businesses (5-100 employees)

  - High administrative costs

## Product Offering

### Six AI Agents

1. **Reception Agent**
   - Answers calls and customer inquiries
   - Available 24/7
   - Natural language processing

2. **Sales Agent**
   - Qualifies leads automatically
   - Nurtures prospects
   - Tracks sales pipeline

3. **Scheduling Agent**
   - Books appointments
   - Manages calendars
   - Sends reminders

4. **Support Agent**
   - Resolves customer issues
   - AI-powered troubleshooting
   - Knowledge base integration

5. **Marketing Agent**
   - Runs automated campaigns
   - Manages follow-ups
   - Email/SMS marketing

6. **Analytics Agent**
   - Business insights
   - Real-time reporting
   - Performance tracking
